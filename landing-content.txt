FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
1. What is petrol pump management software?
Petrol pump management software is a digital platform that helps fuel station owners manage
daily sales, nozzle readings, fuel inventory, accounting, cash collections, employees, customers
and business reports from one system.
2. Which petrol pump brands does Petrozz support?
Petrozz is designed for fuel station operations across IndianOil, BPCL, HPCL, Nayara Energy,
Jio-bp, Shell and independent petrol pumps.
3. Can Petrozz generate a Daily Sales Report (DSR)?
Petrozz provides daily sales and DSR management capabilities, including nozzle-wise readings,
fuel quantities, payment records and shift-wise summaries.
4. Can I manage petrol and diesel separately?
Yes. Petrozz supports product-wise fuel records so that petrol, diesel and other configured fuel
products can be tracked separately.
5. Can I manage multiple fuel tanks?
Petrozz supports tank-wise inventory records, dip readings, stock calculations and reconciliation
for configured fuel tanks.
6. Can Petrozz calculate daily profit and loss?
Petrozz is designed to provide daily financial summaries using recorded sales, purchase costs,
margins and expenses. The accuracy of the result depends on complete and correctly
configured financial records.
7. Does Petrozz have cash drawer management?
Yes. Cash management includes opening and closing balances, cash collections, withdrawals,
expenses, shift handovers and expected-versus-actual cash reconciliation.
8. Can I track POS settlements through Petrozz?
Petrozz is designed to support payment and POS settlement reconciliation through supported
provider integrations, subject to API access and permissions.
9. Which payment providers can be integrated?
Potential integration targets include Paytm, SBI, ICICI and Pine Labs. Actual availability
depends on the provider's supported APIs and account access.
10. Does Petrozz support OCR-based nozzle readings?
Petrozz is designed to use OCR to extract supported nozzle reading information from images.
Extracted values should be reviewed before being saved to operational records.
11. Can I manage credit customers?
Yes. Petrozz supports customer accounts, credit sales, outstanding balances, collections, due
dates and customer transaction histories.
12. Can Petrozz manage employee attendance and
payroll?
Petrozz includes staff management capabilities designed to organise employee records,
attendance, shifts, salary calculations and payroll summaries.
13. Can I manage fuel purchases and suppliers?
Yes. You can maintain supplier information, record fuel purchases, manage purchase invoices
and track supplier-related transactions.
14. Does Petrozz support GST management?
Petrozz is designed to maintain applicable GST and tax-related records. Tax treatment depends
on the product and transaction. Petrol and diesel motor fuels are currently outside the GST levy
in India.
15. Can Petrozz integrate with Tally?
Petrozz is designed to support Tally-compatible accounting workflows and data exchange,
depending on the integration method and supported Tally version.
16. Can I generate QR codes on invoices?
Petrozz is designed to support QR code generation on supported invoices. The QR code's
purpose and format depend on the configured invoicing workflow.
17. Can customers receive bills through WhatsApp?
Petrozz is designed to support digital bill sharing and customer notifications through supported
WhatsApp and SMS integrations.
18. Can Petrozz send lubricant change reminders?
Yes. Customer and vehicle records can be used to schedule lubricant change and service
reminders through supported notification channels.
19. Can I monitor multiple petrol pump outlets?
Petrozz is designed to support multi-outlet management, allowing business owners to view
location-wise operational and financial information through a centralised system.
20. Can I access petrol pump reports remotely?
Petrozz is designed to provide centralised access to business reports and operational
information through its supported platform and user permissions.
21. Is Petrozz suitable for independent petrol pumps?
Yes. Petrozz is designed to support independent fuel stations as well as stations operating
under major oil marketing brands.

Does Petrozz support daily sales reports?

Can I manage multiple fuel tanks and nozzles?

Can I calculate daily petrol pump profit and loss?

margins and expenses.
Does Petrozz support cash drawer management?

handovers and cash reconciliation.
Can I integrate POS payment systems?
Petrozz is designed to support integrations with selected payment providers, subject to their
available APIs and account permissions.
Does Petrozz support Tally?

Can I manage employee attendance and payroll?

reporting.
